# AI Policy
I really dont like AI.
This project was started as an effort to study and learn about the DS hardware, and also for me to refine my coding skills.
Use of AI kinda goes against that...
And frankly, given how often I see cool new projects (or even old ones!) and the crushing disappointment I feel inside me when I learn they were made using AI, I'm inclined to adopt a policy of stubborn rejection of any and all AI usage.
In short: any AI usage will be frowned upon and rejected.
